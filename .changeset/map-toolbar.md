Maps: the tool rail is split into groups with each tool's options beside it; tokens (player, enemy, NPC) and the scene light get their own floating docks. Rooms can keep their own walls inside other rooms and set their own light level, and named rooms show their name. Snapping is per tool (paint and decoration no longer snap), things outside the map can be grabbed, right click cancels a drag in progress, and Play opens a map as the players see it instead of a separate window.
