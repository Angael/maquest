Maps get their own view and tabs: a Maps list to create, open, rename, duplicate and delete maps, one tab per open map, maps in the sidebar, and an "Open as player" window that shows only what the players see. The editor separates the selection inspector, the fog/tokens/layers panel and a Map settings popover; new maps start from project-wide defaults (units, diagonal rule, look). New eraser tool, right click cancels drawing, and handles on the map resize tokens and their vision, lights, doors and labels. Walls can have materials and solid tops (roofs, rock) that players glimpse at the edge of the fog. Fixes vision that went blank at some positions and light leaking past angled doors, and number fields that stepped oddly.
