Auto-layout now has a style picker next to its button. The new default, Smart, follows links where cards are connected and packs unlinked cards and groups into a compact grid shaped to fit the screen, so boards no longer turn into one tall column. Grid packs everything in reading order, Flow → and Flow ↓ follow links (Flow → wraps long chains), and Classic keeps the previous layout. The chosen style is remembered.
