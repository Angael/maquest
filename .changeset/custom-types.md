Node types are now configurable per project. A new Types view lets you rename any type, change its color and pick its icon from the full Lucide set, and add your own entity types (for example "Monster") with starter fields and body. Custom types get their own folder and sidebar section, show up in the canvas filters and "Promote to…" menus, and convert to and from existing types like notes. Agents can manage types through the MCP `project` tool, and `overview` lists them.
